## Rubric coverage and remaining technical limits

| Area | Current coverage | Limit / useful next improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breadth / problem fit | All four task-specific models and outputs | Their official held-out scores remain unknown. |
| Benchmarking and selection | Candidate comparisons, fold/group methods and local metrics retained | Development scores were used for selection; repeated checks do not create an independent final test. |
| Explainability | Measurements, signal previews, prediction records, source provenance and cited AI investigation | No computed per-prediction feature contributions. Descriptive measurements do not establish why the estimator chose its output. |
| Forecasting | Current-condition classification/ranking and cumulative-damage regression | No validated future-failure or remaining-life forecasting model. Forecasting is mentioned in the broad Problem Fit rubric, but is not a required field in the four prediction files. Suitable longitudinal outcomes would be needed to validate it. |
| Ease of use | Unified workflow, visible upload, result-first summaries, one selectable signal chart, 3D evidence and optional AI window | The compulsory demo still needs to show this clearly. No formal non-technical user study or judge usability score is available. |
| Export reliability | Exact schemas and user-selected runs/files, with unavailable-selection checks | An explicit full-official-Test coverage checklist would help prevent accidentally submitting a custom subset or uploaded Train run. A valid custom ZIP is not automatically a complete competition submission. |

The 3D views and map are supporting visualisations. The specification does not require live train tracking, verified vehicle geometry or an LLM. The supplied recordings do not establish live Singapore fleet positions.
